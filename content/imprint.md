## Privacy

### Controller

The controller responsible for processing personal data on this website is the person identified in
the imprint above. Privacy-related questions and requests may be sent to the listed email address.

### Hosting with GitHub Pages

This website is hosted with GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub. When someone visits the
website, GitHub records the visitor's IP address and technical request information for security and
service operation. I do not independently collect or evaluate these access logs.

This processing serves the legitimate interest of providing the website reliably and securely and is
based on Article 6(1)(f) of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Details about GitHub's
processing, retention, and international data transfers are available in the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
and the
[GitHub Pages documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection).

### External resources from jsDelivr

The website downloads MathJax from the jsDelivr content delivery network to display mathematical
notation. Pages containing Mermaid diagrams also download Mermaid from jsDelivr. When these
resources are requested, the visitor's browser connects to `cdn.jsdelivr.net`. According to
jsDelivr, request data may include the IP address, browser type and version, referrer domain,
requested CDN URL, request time, device identifiers, and diagnostic data.

The processing serves the legitimate interest of presenting mathematical notation and diagrams
correctly and is based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Further information is available in the
[jsDelivr Privacy Policy](https://www.jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy).

### Theme preference and local storage

This website does not include first-party analytics or advertising. Its own code does not set
cookies. When a visitor chooses a light or dark color theme, the selected value is stored under the
key `theme` in the browser's local storage. The setting remains on the visitor's device until it is
deleted and is not transmitted to me. It is used only to restore the visitor's chosen appearance on
later visits.

### Contact by email

When someone contacts me by email, I process the sender's address, the message, and associated
technical metadata in order to answer the inquiry. Processing is based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR and
my legitimate interest in responding to messages. If the communication concerns a contract or steps
requested before entering into a contract, processing is instead based on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

### Rights of data subjects

Subject to the applicable legal conditions, visitors have the right to request access to,
rectification or erasure of, and restriction of processing of their personal data. They may also
have a right to data portability and a right to object to processing based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.
Requests may be sent to the email address listed in the imprint.

Visitors also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory
authority under Article 77 GDPR. Information about the supervisory authority in Baden-Württemberg is
available from the
[State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information](https://www.baden-wuerttemberg.datenschutz.de/).
